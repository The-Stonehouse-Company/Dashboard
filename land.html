Nashville

Metro Nashville zoning framework, standard residential setbacks, and the Metro parcel viewer. Verify current values with Metro Codes before contracting — this is a working reference, not authoritative.

Common Residential Zoning

ZoneMin LotTypical Use
RS4040,000 sfLarge-lot single-family
RS2020,000 sfEstate single-family
RS1515,000 sfSingle-family
RS1010,000 sfSingle-family
RS7.57,500 sfSuburban single-family
RS5 / R65,000 – 6,000 sfUrban single-family
R8 / R108,000 – 10,000 sfTwo-family + single-family
RM (multi-family)variesDuplex / apartment

Typical Setbacks (verify with zone)

SetbackRange
Front20 – 40 ft (contextual overlays common)
Rear20 ft typical
Interior side5 – 10 ft
Corner side10 – 20 ft
Max height35 – 45 ft residential

Overlays (HPZ, UDO, DTC, NCZO) impose additional restrictions. Contextual overlays match neighboring setbacks.

Not sure? Email zoninghelpdesk@nashville.gov

What Can Be Built · Quick Rules

  • Detached single-family in all RS/R zones.
  • Duplex requires R8+ (or SP zoning).
  • HPRs allow attached single-family on split-lot conditions.
  • Short-term rental (STRP) categories: Owner-Occupied vs Not — check current caps.
  • Max lot coverage typically 40 – 60% depending on zone.
  • Historic Preservation Zoning (HPZ) districts require Historic Zoning Commission approval for exterior changes.

  • Metro Zoning Code (Municode)
  • Davidson County Tax Records (PADCTN) — search by address to see assessment + flood zone
  • FEMA Flood Map Service — official flood-zone lookup
  • Metro Parcel Viewer (opens below)

Research · Davidson County

Oak Hill

Incorporated satellite city within Davidson County — separate zoning code from Metro Nashville. Large-lot residential character strictly protected; no commercial or multi-family zoning. Verify with Oak Hill Building Commissioner before contracting.

Zoning Districts

ZoneMin LotNotes
R-4040,000 sf (~0.92 ac)Most of Oak Hill
R-3030,000 sf (~0.69 ac)Certain established areas

Single-family only — no duplexes, no ADUs (as of last check).

Typical Setbacks

SetbackR-40 typical
Front50 ft
Rear50 ft
Sides25 ft each
Max height35 ft (verify)
Max coverageLimited — check zone

Setbacks are significantly larger than Metro Nashville. Some historic lots may be non-conforming.

Not sure? Email cityadmin@oakhilltn.us

Build Considerations

  • Every project requires review by the Oak Hill Building Commissioner.
  • Design standards enforce established residential character.
  • Tree preservation ordinances typically apply — mature trees require permits.
  • No STRPs allowed (verify current ordinance).
  • Building permits issued by Oak Hill directly, not Metro Codes.

Links & Reference

  • City of Oak Hill
  • Metro Parcel Viewer (Oak Hill is in Davidson)
  • Davidson County Tax Records (PADCTN) — search for flood zone + assessment
  • FEMA Flood Map Service

Forest Hills

Incorporated satellite city within Davidson County — own zoning, own building official. Similar large-lot residential character to Oak Hill. Verify all zoning + setbacks with Forest Hills before contracting.

Zoning Districts

ZoneMin LotNotes

Research · Williamson County

Brentwood

City of Brentwood — own zoning code administered by Brentwood Planning & Codes. Large-lot residential ethos with OSRD (Open Space Residential Development) as a common tool for subdivisions.

Zoning Districts

ZoneMin LotNotes
R-1~25,000 sf (0.57 ac)Single-family
R-2~15,000 sf (0.34 ac)Single-family (rare in Brentwood)
OSRD-11 unit / 1 ac grossOpen-space subdivision
OSRD-21 unit / 2 ac grossMore rural
OSRD-31 unit / 3 ac grossEstate-rural

Most new Brentwood subdivisions use OSRD to preserve open space in exchange for clustering.

Typical Setbacks (R-1)

SetbackRange
Front50 ft
Rear40 ft
Sides25 ft each
Max height35 ft residential
Max coverage25 – 30%

Build Considerations

  • Brentwood requires Site Plan approval for most builds.
  • Steep slopes, karst, and sinkhole geology — geotech often required.
  • Tree Preservation Ordinance applies.
  • Extensive stormwater / drainage review.
  • OSRD projects require concurrent open-space dedication.
  • Permits issued by Brentwood Codes Department.

Links & Reference

  • Brentwood Planning & Codes
  • Williamson County GIS Viewer
  • Williamson County Tax Records (Property Assessor) — search for flood zone + assessment
  • FEMA Flood Map Service
